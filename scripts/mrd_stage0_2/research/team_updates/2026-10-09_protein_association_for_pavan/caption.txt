Surface-protein correlations with the RNA-only PC8 score are small and mostly reflect cell type.

- PC8: a per-cell score from a factor model fit on RNA only (the 8th principal component of the Hallmark interferon-alpha response genes). It is oriented so that positive values point toward the pole where malignant cells sit. Surface protein was not used to build it.
- Protein: raw CITE-seq antibody counts, transformed per cell by a centered log-ratio (CLR): log(1 + count) minus that cell's mean log(1 + count) over all 170 antibodies (161 targets and 9 isotype controls).
- Panel A marks: Spearman rank correlation (rho) between PC8 score and protein level, computed separately in normal and in malignant cells. Open square = all cells of a label pooled (the values in our earlier table). Dot = median over the 7 patients with at least 10 malignant cells; line = range across them; right column = patients whose rho has the median's sign. Diamond = median over patient x predicted cell type groups of at least 30 cells (71 groups from all 7 patients for normal cells; 11 groups from P02, P03, P04 and P09 only for malignant cells).
- Proteins shown: the 5 with the largest within-patient median, the 3 from our earlier table (bold), and any protein that meets our pre-set rule (within-patient median |rho| >= 0.20, same sign in at least 5 of 7 patients) and keeps at least half of that inside cell types (this adds only CD82).
- Isotype floor: the largest |rho| that any of the 9 isotype-control antibodies (no specific target) reaches with PC8 at the same level, i.e. how much correlation non-specific antibody background alone produces. It is a rough guide, not a significance test. Dots inside the gray band cannot be told apart from background. Compare squares with the dashed lines (pooled floor) and diamonds with the printed within-cell-type floor (0.04 normal, 0.12 malignant).
- Normal cells: CD34 rises with PC8 in all 7 patients (median rho +0.245) but hardly within a cell type (+0.019). The other proteins shown also shrink to |rho| <= 0.063 within cell types. This fits PC8 differing between cell types: among normal cells it is highest in EBM, MEP-MkP and HSC/MPP1 (panel B).
- Malignant cells: estimates are noisier (22 to 758 malignant cells per patient). The within-patient floor is 0.23 (vs 0.11 in normal cells); one isotype control sets it, and the other eight stay at or below 0.16. Four of the strongest proteins (CD48, CD116, CD33, CD146) fall to |rho| <= 0.04 within cell types. CD63 (-0.252 within patient, 6/7; -0.138 within cell type) and CD82 (+0.238, 7/7; +0.143) keep more than half. CD99, the top protein of our earlier table, is weak: +0.159 within patients (6/7, below the 0.23 floor) and +0.150 within cell types (same sign in 9 of 11 groups). These three sit just above the 0.12 within-cell-type floor, which the same isotype control reaches, so they are suggestive at most.
- Panel B: mean PC8 score per predicted cell type (in brackets: malignant cells). Malignant cells score higher than normal cells of the same type in 8 of 10 types with patients pooled, and in 19 of 20 within-patient comparisons in the types shown (19 of 21 including pDC2, not shown). Right column: - = no patient with at least 10 cells of each label.
- FA7, the factor-analysis version in our earlier table, gives the same picture (normal-cell CD34 +0.298 within patients, 7/7; -0.004 within cell types). Its numbers are in the attached table.
- All results are descriptive.
