## Claim gate

Spearman rho is the correlation of two per-cell rank vectors computed within one label; its sign says whether the oriented factor ranking and the protein ranking run the same way or in reverse. In malignant cells, CD48, CD116, CD33 and CD146 track PC8 within patients (robust rule met) but not inside predicted cell types; CD99 does not meet the rule yet keeps its size inside cell types over 11 groups from 4 patients; CD63 and CD82 do both. Figure 19's pooled means mix patients (P03 holds 68% of the malignant HSC/MPP2 cells).

Does not support:
- any surface protein as a malignancy marker or a single-cell marker of PC8 (the CD99 result is a lead, not a finding);
- a malignant-vs-healthy contrast from any rho (each rho is within one label);
- reading the isotype bands or the robust rule as significance tests;
- separating patient from batch, or malignancy from recipient origin;
- conclusions about dsb-normalized ADT;
- integration of protein into the factors (there is none);
- causal or mechanistic readings; inductive deployment (Stage 1 is transductive); recurrence risk.
